# Ecosystem Conformance

RITES is the Carbon Actual human continuity infrastructure/product.

## Owns
Identity-context continuity workflows, relationships, wishes, responsibilities, transitions, digital estate, succession, ceremony/rite, archives and legacy.

## Consumes
Canonical `#` identity, authority/SEAL, consent, proof/provenance, events, resources, services, value and adapters.

## Boundary
RITES does not create a competing identity, authority, ledger, banking, governance or constitutional ontology. It composes canonical ecosystem primitives for continuity use cases.

## Key rule
Continuity state is not authority. A record, wish, relationship or scenario becomes actionable only through the applicable authority, consent, evidence and policy checks.
