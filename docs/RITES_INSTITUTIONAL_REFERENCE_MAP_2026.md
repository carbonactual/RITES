# RITES Institutional Reference Map — 2026-09-19

RITES is a Carbon Actual continuity product, not a government authority. There is no single Nigerian institution that lawfully owns the whole RITES domain. RITES therefore routes each consequential matter to the competent authority for that specific function and jurisdiction.

| Continuity function | Primary institutional reference | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| National identity | National Identity Management Commission (NIMC) | NIN, identity credentials, national digital identity, authentication/trust reference |
| Birth/death civil registration | National Population Commission (NPC) plus applicable state/local registration workflow | Vital-event registration and official civil records |
| Personal data/privacy | Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) | Privacy and data-protection compliance; RITES does not create privacy law |
| Probate/estate administration | Relevant state judiciary/probate registry | Probate procedure is jurisdiction-specific; Lagos cannot be generalized to every state |
| Corporate/business continuity | Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) | Company registration/status; not proof of ownership of every underlying asset |
| Financial execution | Competent licensed financial institution / CBN or SEC-regulated provider as applicable | RITES may coordinate information and instructions; it does not silently execute transfers |
| Religious/cultural rites | The relevant religious institution, custodian or family-authorized authority | Belief/tradition is not converted into universal legal authority |
| Health/care records | The relevant health institution and competent regulator; MDCN for regulated medical/dental practitioners | RITES may carry instructions/relationships but does not infer diagnosis or medical authority |
| Cemetery/mortuary | Competent local/state authority and service provider | No invented national RITES registry |

## Missing-specialist rule

Where no single specialized institution exists for a continuity function, ABBA uses the Carbon Actual institutional registry to compose function-specific authorities. It must label the jurisdiction, source type and uncertainty rather than inventing a universal authority.

## Authority boundary

A source can provide evidence, procedure, availability or institutional context. It does not automatically authorize RITES, ABBA or an agent to act. High-impact actions continue through identity → authority → consent → capability → workflow → execution → evidence.
