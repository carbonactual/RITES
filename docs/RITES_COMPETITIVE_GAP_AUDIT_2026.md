# RITES Competitive Gap Audit — 19 August 2026

## Market finding

The market is strong in categories but fragmented across them.

- Trustworthy positions as a family operating system with encrypted vault, AI answers, permissions, reminders, legacy access and concierge tiers.
- GoodTrust combines will/trust/directives, digital vault, funeral directive, pet directive and trusted-contact workflows.
- Everplans organizes documents, family/home/health/money/digital estate and end-of-life plans and also serves professional advisors.
- Ever Loved combines funeral planning, memorial websites, event RSVPs, livestreams, flowers, donations, photos, stories and provider discovery.
- Find a Grave/BillionGraves specialize in memorial/grave discovery and mapped cemetery information.
- FamilySearch/Ancestry/MyHeritage specialize in genealogy, records, family trees, DNA and increasingly AI-assisted historical-document interpretation.
- Apple/Google provide provider-specific legacy account controls.
- Science Care provides whole-body donation coordination.
- PlotBox and the broader funeral/cemetery software market cover provider operations such as mapping, records, work orders, scheduling, financials and document management.

## What RITES must not imitate

RITES should not rebuild every specialist database or pretend to be the legal/medical/civil authority. It should integrate authoritative and specialist systems through adapters and own the cross-domain Continuum.

## Defensible RITES gap

`Intention -> Continuum -> Contextual authority -> Event -> Handoff -> Service -> Economic fulfillment -> Verified outcome -> Long-run continuation`

Competitors generally own a subset of that chain. RITES is designed to connect the chain.

## Additional capabilities now canonical

1. **Life archive**: diaries, journals, handwriting, signatures, certificates, licenses, awards, photographs, negatives, slides, audio, video, letters, books, artwork, recipes, memorabilia, clothing, medals, heirlooms, objects, stories and oral history.
2. **Identity & lineage**: genealogy, family, household, community, culture, migration, military/veteran history, rank, profession, specialties, gifts, accessibility and relationships.
3. **Evidence continuum**: provenance, confidence, verification state, disputed state and authoritative source.
4. **Digital estate**: account inventory, device continuity, public/private/media scrub, provider-specific handoff, memorialization, transfer, archive, closure and deletion.
5. **Medical/care continuity**: allergies, medications, care preferences, clinicians, pharmacy, serious illness and emergency context under strict authorization.
6. **Make-a-Wish / meaningful-life planning**: an intention and experience layer for serious illness and other major life circumstances, with appropriate human-care boundaries.
7. **Ceremony orchestration**: rites, officiants, eulogy, music, speakers, flowers, clothing, transport, venue, cemetery, media, guests, remote participation, printed materials and post-event tasks.
8. **Mortuary and death transition**: reported event, verification, identification, mortuary, funeral director, transport, legal/medical authority, disposition, cemetery and long-run memorial state.
9. **Grave continuum**: find, register, locate, visit, maintain, flowers, memorial, anniversary and event scheduling.
10. **Affairs & obligations**: who owes, who is owed, debts, commitments, associations, savings groups, business obligations and unresolved matters.
11. **Marketplace**: provider discovery, qualification, offer, booking, payment/provision, fulfillment, evidence and settlement.
12. **Scenario simulation**: self-planning, incapacity, serious illness, sudden death, death abroad, child/infant, pregnancy loss, pet, military/veteran, public official/VVIP, caregiver, executor, faith leader, business succession and provider operations.
13. **Multi-role continuity**: one person can simultaneously be self, child, parent, spouse, caregiver, executor, professional, religious leader, government worker or provider; each role produces a distinct authorized view.
14. **Curated Continuum**: the long-run synthesis of what was intended, what was handed off, what was fulfilled and what continues.

## New competitive principle

Specialist capability should be **consumed, connected and contextualized**, not copied.

RITES should win on:

- breadth without flattening domains
- contextual permissions instead of simple family sharing
- event-driven continuity instead of static vaults
- intention-to-fulfillment workflows instead of storage alone
- multi-role experiences instead of one dashboard
- long-run continuity instead of ending at the funeral
- provider/government/religious/legal integration instead of siloing
- provenance and verification instead of treating user-entered claims as facts
- AI curation with bounded authority instead of unrestricted autonomy

## Commercial moat

The moat is not a cemetery database or vault size. It is the Continuum graph plus authority model, event history, handoff network, provider ecosystem, institutional adapters, long-run memories and accumulated verified relationships.
