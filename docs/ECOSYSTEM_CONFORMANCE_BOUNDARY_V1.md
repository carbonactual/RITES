# Ecosystem Conformance Boundary V1

RITES is the continuity operating environment of Carbon Actual.

RITES may own continuity-specific workflows, records and operational behavior, but universal identity, authority, provenance, capability, event and value semantics come from OMNII and the Carbon Actual estate contracts.

RITES must preserve continuity history, explicit recovery states, evidence and authority boundaries. It must not become a competing constitution or financial authority.
