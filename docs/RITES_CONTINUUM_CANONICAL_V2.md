# RITES Continuum — Canonical V2

RITES is Human Continuity Infrastructure. The product is not a funeral app, genealogy database, digital vault, cemetery directory, estate tool, or marketplace in isolation. It is the orchestration layer connecting identity, relationships, intentions, life records, heritage, events, authority, services, economy, handoffs and long-run continuity.

## Canonical graph

Person -> relationships -> roles -> places -> events -> intentions -> records -> assets -> obligations -> providers -> authorities -> outcomes -> future events.

Every object carries provenance, sensitivity, authority, state and access policy.

## Individual experience

There is no universal dashboard. RITES renders a contextual Continuum for each authorized user based on their roles, relationships, responsibilities, active scenarios, geography, timing and permissions. One person may simultaneously be self-planning, a child of a deceased person, caregiver, executor, religious leader, government employee, professional provider and curator for another person.

## Life and identity

Support identity, names, aliases, lineage, genealogy, family, household, community, culture, language, faith, moral stands, core beliefs, accessibility needs, rank, profession, service history, military/veteran status, achievements, certifications, licenses, awards, specialties, gifts, talents, interests, activities, travel, education, friendships, relationships, pets and animals.

## Life record and biography

The Continuum may preserve a life timeline, diaries, journals, handwriting, signatures, letters, certificates, school records, professional records, medical records where authorized, therapy records where authorized, medication information where authorized, allergies, preferences, disliked things, favorite foods, clothing, products, sports, arts, creations, poems, recipes, stories, accomplishments, KPIs, travel, friendships, unbelievable moments, struggles, happiness, values, advice, confessions and experiences.

Claims are never silently converted to facts. Verification and provenance are first-class.

## Heritage and physical memory

Heritage items include physical photographs, negatives, slides, albums, videos, audio, books, artwork, manuscripts, memorabilia, medals, uniforms, jewelry, heirlooms, objects, clothing, trophies, awards, statues/figures, framed photographs, letters and other tangible records. Each item can be digitized, described, preserved, transferred, exhibited, gifted, buried/disposed where lawful, or kept private.

## Genealogy and DNA

Genealogy and DNA are adapter domains. RITES can connect authorized family trees, historical records and genetic relationship evidence without pretending to be the authoritative source. Genetic information is highly sensitive and requires explicit consent, purpose limitation, provenance and strict access controls.

## Health and serious illness

Support authorized care context including allergies, medication, therapy, clinicians, pharmacies, symptoms, diagnoses and care preferences. Medical facts remain under appropriate clinical authority. Serious/terminal illness supports a Make a Wish continuum for meaningful experiences, people, places, creations and unfinished intentions, with age-appropriate and care-team-aware workflows.

## Before / during / after / long run

Before: curate, plan, protect, provision, document, rehearse scenarios.
During: verify, notify, coordinate, protect, hand off, conduct rites.
After: settle, transfer, preserve, memorialize, fulfill.
Long run: anniversaries, grave visits, maintenance, timed messages, family milestones, promises, provisions, genealogy, archives and future activations.

## Death and mortuary continuum

Model reported death, competent confirmation, identification evidence, official registration, morgue/mortuary, mortician/funeral director, transport, religious/cultural rites, cemetery/cremation, exhumation cases, autopsy/legal processes, donation for science, organ/tissue donation where applicable, burial/disposition and family handoffs. RITES coordinates; competent authorities certify, authorize and decide.

## Ceremony and publication

A Ceremony Composer controls officiant, faith, readings, eulogy, music, band, procession, transport, clothing, coffin/casket preferences, flowers, photographs, video, livestream, printed flyers, souvenirs, venue, grave and roles.

A Publication Gate controls who is told first, who may publish, audience, framing, exact media, RIP treatment, captions, timing, embargoes, private-only material and prohibited media. The system may recommend; sensitive public release requires authority.

Attendance is a priority graph: must, explicitly wished, committed, priority, invited, optional, remote, unavailable. RITES coordinates alternatives but cannot guarantee another person's attendance.

## Confidential Continuum

Secrets, confessions, private letters, affairs, estranged relationships, disputed family claims, private advice and sensitive material can be sealed with recipient, condition, trigger, authority, release window and fallback. Death does not automatically unlock a secret.

Potential offspring/heir claims remain claims until appropriately verified. Legal inheritance decisions remain with competent legal authorities.

## Digital estate

Inventory social profiles, email, domains, cloud storage, code/data, devices, financial profiles and other digital assets. Each has preservation, memorialization, transfer, closure, archive, deletion or no-action intent plus scrub and publication policies. RITES records provider-specific handoff requirements rather than pretending to control third-party accounts.

## Economy and marketplace

Need -> request -> provider -> offer -> agreement -> booking -> payment/provision -> fulfillment -> evidence -> settlement -> rating/outcome. Supports mortuary, cemetery, transport, printing, flowers, ceremony, legal, medical, genealogy, archival, memorial, maintenance, travel, accessibility and other services.

## Intelligence and agents

Agents are scoped operators, not autonomous owners of the Continuum. Agent capabilities include discovery, classification, curation, summarization, gap detection, scenario simulation, provider matching, workflow coordination, reporting and recommendation. High-impact actions require configured human/institutional approval.

Core loop: detect -> understand -> curate -> prioritize -> recommend -> authorize -> execute -> verify -> record -> continue.

## Reporting

Generate role-specific views and reports for individual/family, executor, caregiver, provider, cemetery, mortuary, religious institution, legal, medical/public-health, government, business and authorized researchers. Every report carries audience, scope, provenance and audit history.

## Guardrails

Separate wish from authority, claim from fact, memory from evidence, recommendation from action, private from public, family preference from legal requirement and AI inference from verified information. Irreversible or high-impact actions require explicit authority and evidence. Minimum-necessary access is mandatory.

## Architecture

Supabase is the persistence and policy layer. Vercel hosts the application and edge-facing experience. GitHub is the canonical source repository and CI/CD control plane. External genealogy, DNA, government, health, digital-account and specialist providers are adapters. The frontend is a contextual renderer over the Continuum graph, not the source of truth.

## Product thesis

RITES does not merely record what happened. It understands what matters, what was intended, what changed, what must be handed off, what remains unresolved and what should continue.
