# Ecosystem Conformance

RITES is the Carbon Actual human continuity infrastructure/product.

## Owns
Identity-context continuity workflows, relationships, wishes, responsibilities, transitions, digital estate, succession, ceremony/rite, archives and legacy.

## Consumes
Canonical `#` identity, authority/SEAL, consent, proof/provenance, events, resources, services, value and adapters.

## Orchestration
**ABBA** is the ecosystem orchestration/intelligence layer for RITES. RITES sends governed continuity intent/context to the existing authenticated ABBA control-plane endpoint. ABBA can analyze, curate, recommend and prepare; it does not own RITES continuity data and it cannot issue authority, replace SEAL/consent, or silently execute consequential continuity actions.

## Boundary
RITES does not create a competing identity, authority, ledger, banking, governance or constitutional ontology. It composes canonical ecosystem primitives for continuity use cases. ABBA does not become a RITES-owned authority primitive; it remains an ecosystem orchestrator operating inside the canonical OMNII authority model.

## Key rule
Continuity state is not authority. A record, wish, relationship or scenario becomes actionable only through the applicable authority, consent, evidence and policy checks. ABBA recommendations never infer consent from continuity state.
